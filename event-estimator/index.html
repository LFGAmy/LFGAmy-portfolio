Copy it into your event brief, or save the link to come back after the event. The gap between the forecast and the result is what tells you which formats to run again. Doing that across a whole calendar of events is what I'm building Dott for.

How to use this honestly

This is a forecast, not tracking. Nothing here connects to your data, and nothing you enter is saved. After the event, check each step in your own tools: RSVPs and check-ins from your event page, joins from Discord or Slack, signups or projects from your product, opportunities from your CRM. Count an outcome as influenced, not sourced, and only when you can actually tie it back to the event, for example with a tagged event link or a check-in list. Reach and share of voice are a separate layer. Method: Plan → Forecast → Prove → Score, the operating model behind Dott.

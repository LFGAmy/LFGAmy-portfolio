## Events are the real-world trust layer

If you build an event and no one shows up, you don't have an event. And if you build one where all you're thinking about is what you'll gain — not what you'll give — you've wasted time and money. Either way, it comes back to the same thing: the people are the event. You need the people to fill the room. You need the people to provide the data. You need the people for the conversion.

So the promise is simple. Stop thinking about what you need, and start thinking about what you can give. Trust is built when the service runs both ways — and it lives in the details and the care you put into the experience.
